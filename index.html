Educational Leader and Researcher

Bridging scientific inquiry with classroom leadership.

I am an Educational Leader at Mount Waverley Primary School in Victoria, Australia, and an education researcher. Bridging a background in research science (M.Sc. Biochemistry) with school leadership (M.Teach), I focus on student wellbeing, evidence-based pedagogy, and instructional coaching.

As an alumnus of the ACEL NextGen initiative, I write and speak on fostering educator co-agency and designing learner-centered educational environments.
