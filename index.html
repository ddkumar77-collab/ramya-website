ACADEMIC INQUIRY

PhD Research · University of Canberra

Enacting Learner Agency in Primary Classrooms

My PhD research at the University of Canberra explores how learner agency is experienced, expressed and developed in primary classrooms.

I am particularly interested in the relationship between what learners are capable of doing, the opportunities classrooms provide for them to act, and the processes through which agency develops over time.

Rather than treating agency simply as a characteristic that students possess, my research asks what agency looks like when it is enacted within the real conditions of classroom life.
